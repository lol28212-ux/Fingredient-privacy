Privacy Policy for Fingredient

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Fingredient has no accounts, no logins, and no analytics. Your allergen and dietary preferences stay on your device. Photos of ingredient labels are read on your phone and are never uploaded.

Fingredient helps you check whether a food product contains ingredients you are avoiding. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information stored on your device

Your watch list. The ingredients and custom terms you choose to avoid are saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. This information is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Scan results. Results are held in memory while you use the app and are not uploaded.

Camera and photos

Fingredient requests camera access so you can scan a barcode or an ingredients label, and photo library access so you can choose a label photo you already took.

Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework. Photos and the text extracted from them are never uploaded, stored on a server, or shared with anyone. They are discarded when you leave the scan.

Information sent off your device

When you scan a barcode, Fingredient sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free and open food product database, in order to look up the product's ingredients. No information about you, your device, or your watch list is included in that request.

Open Food Facts is an independent third party and handles requests under its own privacy policy.

This is the only network request the app makes.

What Fingredient does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No selling or sharing of personal information
  • No collection of location, contacts, health records, or device identifiers

Children

Fingredient does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Fingredient is a reading aid, not a medical device. Label text can be misread, incomplete, or out of date, and product formulations change. Always read the physical package and follow guidance from your doctor or allergist. Do not rely on this app alone to avoid an ingredient that could harm you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
